What is Wushu?

The art behind the sport

Wushu (武术) is the modern, standardized form of Chinese martial arts. It blends deep stances, explosive jumps, and flowing weapon work into choreographed routines called forms, or taolu.

Taolu is what our club focuses on: it is performative and non-contact, judged on difficulty, execution, and presentation rather than fighting. Alongside barehand forms, practitioners train traditional weapons such as staff, broadsword, straightsword, and spear.
