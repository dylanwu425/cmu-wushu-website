How to join

There’s no application, no tryout, and no dues — anyone can start, at any point in the semester. You don’t have to be a CMU student.

  1. Show up to a practice Check the calendar and come to whichever session fits your week. No sign-up and no experience needed.
  2. Join our Discord This is where we post schedule changes, room changes, and event announcements. Join the server.
