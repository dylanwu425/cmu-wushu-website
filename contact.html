How to join

There’s no application, no tryout, and no dues — anyone can start.

  1. Show up to a practice Check the calendar and come to whichever session fits your week. No sign-up, no dues, and no experience needed.
  2. Join our Discord This is where we post schedule changes, room changes, and event announcements. Join the server.
Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need any martial arts experience?

No. Most of our members started with none at all, and we teach fundamentals every practice.

Do I need to buy my own weapon?

No — the club supplies practice weapons.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There are no dues and practices are free.

Do I have to compete or perform?

There’s no pressure to perform, but performances are welcome and open to all experience levels.

Can I join mid-semester?

Yes. Come to any practice, any week — there’s no cutoff date.

Is this open to non-CMU students?

Yes.
