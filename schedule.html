Your First Practice

What to expect

A typical practice opens with a warm-up activity and stretching. Next is basics: stances, kicks, and jumps. It finishes with form work, teaching, and strength conditioning.

Officers and experienced members guide practice, keeping it a comfortable environment to train and learn in.

No registration is needed for your first visit, so just show up. Joining our Discord is the easiest way to keep up with changes.

What to bring

  • Comfortable athletic clothes you can stretch in
  • Wushu shoes, or train barefoot or in socks
  • A water bottle
  • No equipment needed, the club provides practice weapons
