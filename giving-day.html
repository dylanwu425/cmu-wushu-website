义 Giving CMU Day · December 1, 2026

Practice here has always been free. Keeping it that way costs something.

Anyone can walk into our practice without paying a cent, and we have never wanted that to change. The mats, the loaner weapons, the costumes: it all comes from gifts the size of the ones on this page. Anything from $5 up is a real contribution here — CMU scores the day by how many people give, not how much, so a $10 gift counts for every bit as much as a $500 one.

Why a small gift is not a token gift

Alongside Giving CMU Day, CMU runs matches and challenges for student organizations: the clubs that bring in the most individual donors giving $5 or more win a share of a prize pool on top of whatever they raise. Hundreds of orgs are in that race and we are not counting on winning it. We mention it for what it says about the scoring — the day is counted by people, not by dollars.

So your $10 lands on the board exactly as hard as somebody else’s $500.

One gift per person: CMU counts donors by card and email address, and audits them afterwards. Please don’t give on somebody else’s behalf — ask them to give instead, which is worth more to us anyway.

Where it goes

No overhead, no mystery

We are students. It buys equipment, costumes, and the cost of getting us to a stage.

  • Weapons and mats the club lends out, so nobody has to buy gear before their first practice.
  • Getting people and weapons across Pittsburgh to the festivals and showcases we perform at.
  • Costumes and props for Lunar New Year and the spring showcase.
  • Keeping the barrier at zero. No dues, no equipment list, no tryout — which only works if the gear is already on the floor when someone walks in.
