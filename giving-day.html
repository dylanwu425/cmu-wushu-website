Why $10 is the magic number

CMU runs a Student Organization Challenge alongside Giving CMU Day. The clubs that bring in the most individual donors giving $10 or more take home $6,000, $4,000 and $2,000 on top of whatever they raise. It is counted by heads, not by dollars.

A hundred people giving $10 beats one person giving $1,000 — twice over.

Where it goes

No overhead, no mystery

We are students. The money buys equipment and pays entry fees.

  • Weapons and mats the club lends out, so nobody has to buy gear before their first practice.
  • Registration and travel for the collegiate competition season.
  • Costumes and props for Lunar New Year and the spring showcase.
  • Keeping practice free. We have never charged dues and want to keep it that way.
