Privacy

Wulf’s Playground is a formation planner made for the CMU Wushu club. Last updated November 6, 2026.

What’s stored online

  • Your Google name, email address and profile picture, so club members can see who’s who.
  • Your shows, so they’re on every device you sign in on, with their history: who changed what and when, and saved versions.
  • Clubs you join and your role in them.

This is kept on Supabase, the service the app runs on. Private shows are visible only to you and anyone you invite to that show; club shows to everyone in the club. Music stays on your device unless you choose to upload it for sharing.

Ask AI

When you use Ask AI, your request and a description of the show (performers, positions, formations) are sent to Anthropic's Claude to work out the edit. A daily count of requests is kept to stay within limits.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Google sign-in is only used to know who you are.

Removing your data

Delete a show from your projects page and it’s removed from your account. You can also leave a club, or move a show out of it. To have your account and everything tied to it deleted, ask through the CMU Wushu contact page.
