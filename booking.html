For Event Organizers

Bring wushu to your event

The CMU Wushu Club performs at cultural showcases, campus celebrations, and community events. Our routines are fast, visual, and work well for audiences of any age or background.

🥋

Barehand Forms

Solo or group routines showcasing speed, stances, and acrobatic technique. Typically 1–2 minutes per routine.

⚔️

Weapons Demonstrations

Staff, broadsword, straightsword, and spear routines. Highly visual and usually the audience favorite.

👥

Group Showcases

A choreographed set combining several members and weapon types into one continuous performance.
