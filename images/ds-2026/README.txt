Oops!… It's DS Again — Dancers Symposium
Friday 24 & Saturday 25 April 2026

Put photos for this event in THIS folder.

  1. Drop your image files here (JPG or PNG).
  2. Name them simply and in lowercase, e.g. 01-group-form.jpg
  3. In events.html, find the section headed "Oops!… It's DS Again — Dancers Symposium" and swap a blank
     placeholder tile for your photo. Each tile looks like this:

       <figure class="gallery__item">
         <img src="images/ds-2026/01-group-form.jpg"
              alt="Describe what is happening in the photo"
              loading="lazy">
         <figcaption>Your caption</figcaption>
       </figure>

  4. Always fill in the alt text — it is what blind visitors hear.

Tip: resize photos to about 1600px wide before adding them, so pages load fast.
HEIC files from an iPhone will NOT display in browsers — export as JPEG first.
